# Privacy Policy

> This is a translation for convenience; the Korean version prevails in case of discrepancy.

Effective date: October 5, 2026 · Version 1.0

Baryon Labs (바리온랩스, the "Company") takes care of the personal information of people who use **DJI2Note** (the "App").

## 1. Information the Company receives: anonymous usage stats only
- The Company receives only **anonymous usage stats** (a random install ID, the App version, the macOS version and per-day feature usage counts). It does not receive recordings, transcripts, summaries, meeting titles, file names, accounts, email addresses, names of people or location, and the App has no advertising or tracking.
- Purpose: understanding how features are used and improving the App. The data is not used for other purposes, provided to third parties or sold.
- You can see exactly what is sent, word for word, in the App's About tab, and turn it off at any time (turning it off also deletes records not yet sent). Versions without a destination address send nothing.
- The IP address that reaches the collection server is not stored in the stats or linked to the install ID.
- Stats received are kept for as long as needed to improve the App (up to 2 years) and then deleted.
- Exception: if you choose **Baryon AI** as your AI, the transcript text needed for summaries is sent to the Company's server. The Company uses it only to produce transcript cleanup and summary responses, and does not use it for any other purpose or provide it to third parties.
- If you send us an inquiry by email, we process your email address and message only as needed to reply, and delete them within one year after replying. Email addresses submitted for the Windows version notification are used only for the launch announcement and are deleted after the announcement or on request.

## 2. Information stored on your Mac
Recordings, notes, settings (API keys and tokens) and logs are stored only on your Mac. Their locations are listed in section 5 of the Data and AI notice. The settings file is saved with permissions that only you can read (600).

## 3. External services you choose
Information is sent to the AI you choose (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, etc.) and to the Google Drive, Notion and Zoom accounts you connect, using your own accounts and keys. Its handling is governed by each provider's privacy policy. Everything that is sent is listed in the Data and AI notice.

## 4. Personal information of other participants
Recordings contain other people's voices and statements. You must inform them and obtain their consent before recording, and comply with applicable laws when sharing notes with others.

## 5. Your rights
The only information the Company keeps is anonymous usage stats, inquiry emails and notification sign-up addresses. Stats can be turned off in the About tab. To access, correct or delete it, contact us at the address below. You can delete information stored on your Mac yourself.

## 6. Privacy officer and contact
Baryon Labs · hello@baryon.ai

## 7. Changes
If this policy changes, we will announce it in an App update and on the intro page, and update the effective date and version.
