Sources

New recordings get handled on their own

DJI wireless mics

Plug the transmitter in over USB and the app wakes up, copies the new recordings first, then processes them. You can unplug the mic right away. Recordings split into 30-minute files are joined back into one meeting.

Record in the app

Record with the built-in, USB or iPhone mic, or a DJI receiver. For online meetings it captures your voice and your Mac's audio separately, so "me" and "others" are split accurately.

Zoom

Imports Zoom recordings saved on your Mac and Zoom cloud recordings. When per-participant audio is available, speakers are labeled by name.

Mac Voice Memos

iPhone voice memos that sync to your Mac through iCloud become meeting notes too.

Batch processing

Works through a backlog of recordings without any input from you. Progress and CPU usage show in the menu bar.

On-device transcription

Transcribes on your Mac with mlx-whisper, and filters out the stray sentences Whisper tends to invent during silence.

Situation-specific summaries

A meeting and a lecture need different notes

Install

Three steps and you're done

  1. Install the app

    Paste this into Terminal. It installs the app into Applications → Baryon and opens it right away, with no security warning.

    curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/baryonlabs/dji2note-releases/main/install-app.sh | bash

  2. Setup wizard

    Install the engine → choose a summary AI (Claude login, Codex, API key, Baryon AI) → connect your Google account. No admin password or Homebrew needed.

  3. Plug in the mic

    Connect a DJI mic or start recording in the app. When it's done you get a Mac notification, and you can view or copy the summary and transcript straight from the notes list.

Downloaded the DMG instead? The first time you open it, macOS may warn that it can't verify the app. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and press Open Anyway at the bottom once.

Connections

Notes go where they belong

Google Drive

Uploads the summary and speaker-separated transcript as Google Docs. Connect several accounts and pick which one to use.

Notion

Creates a summary page for each meeting with the transcript as a subpage. Pick a database and the title and date are filled in too.

Work tools (MCP)

Shares organized summaries and context over an MCP server, so a work dashboard or bot can pick them up regularly and turn them into tasks.

Privacy

Your audio never leaves your Mac

Transcription happens on your Mac. For summaries, only the transcript text is sent to the AI you choose (Claude, OpenAI, Gemini and so on), and uploads go only to the Drive or Notion you connected. Baryon Labs receives only anonymous stats such as per-feature usage counts, and you can turn them off in the About tab. Please get consent from the people you're talking with before you record.
